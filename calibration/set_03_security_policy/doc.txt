ENTERPRISE INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version 4.2 - Effective Date: January 15, 2026

1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE
This policy establishes baseline security controls for all corporate infrastructure, cloud environments, employee workstations, and customer data processing pipelines, adhering to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (Security, Availability, Confidentiality) and ISO/IEC 27001 / 27701 frameworks.

2. LOGICAL ACCESS CONTROLS (CC6.1, CC6.2, CC6.3)
- Principle of Least Privilege: Access to production environments is granted strictly on a need-to-know basis.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Enforced for all systems, corporate email, VPN, and AWS/Supabase administrative consoles via FIDO2 hardware tokens or TOTP.
- Credential Management: Password complexity requires a minimum of 16 characters. Shared accounts are strictly prohibited.
- Access Deprovisioning: HR automated webhooks revoke access across all SSO and cloud identity providers within 4 hours of employee departure.

3. NETWORK SECURITY AND PERIMETER PROTECTION (CC6.6, CC6.7)
- Network Segmentation: Production infrastructure is segregated into isolated VPCs with no direct public access to database subnets.
- Firewalls & WAF: Cloudflare WAF and AWS Security Groups restrict ingress traffic to HTTPS (port 443). All administrative ports (SSH, RDP) require encrypted WireGuard bastion access.
- Encryption: All data in transit must utilize TLS 1.3. All persistent data volumes, databases, and S3 vaults are encrypted at rest using AES-256 via customer-managed AWS KMS keys.

4. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT AND BREACH NOTIFICATION (CC7.1, CC7.2, CC7.3)
- The Security Incident Response Team (SIRT) maintains a 24/7 on-call rotation.
- In the event of a confirmed security incident involving personal data, SIRT initiates the Incident Response Playbook and notifies affected customers and regulators within 72 hours per GDPR Article 33.

5. BUSINESS CONTINUITY AND DISASTER RECOVERY (A1.2, A1.3)
- Automated immutable snapshots of production databases are captured every 6 hours and replicated to a secondary AWS geographical region.
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours.
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 1 hour.
- Disaster recovery failover exercises are tested and documented semi-annually.

6. VENDOR AND THIRD-PARTY GOVERNANCE
- All third-party SaaS vendors and sub-processors undergo mandatory security risk assessments prior to onboarding and must hold current SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certifications.
